Today, intori is on the App Store for iPhone.

Most apps that help a family run help with what you have to do: practice, appointments, pickups. Keeping track of the fun stuff is one more job, and it usually falls to one person, or to nobody. intori takes that job.

## What it does

Tell intori what your household loves, once. Then it keeps watch, so you don't have to ask.

- **Sports:** your teams' next games, on your local day and time.
- **Music:** concerts near you from the artists you like, and when tickets go on sale.
- **Shows:** the date your show comes back, and where it is streaming.
- **Food:** dinner spots near the plans you already have.

## One tap to your calendar

Every pick has one button: Add to calendar. Tap it, and the date is on your iPhone's calendar. intori adds only the dates you choose and never reads your other events. Want a nudge? Turn on a reminder: the day before a game or a show, two days before a concert.

## A few good picks, then done

Home shows up to five picks a day, each with the reason it is there. When you have seen them, intori says so. There is no feed to scroll, and no ads. You pay for intori, so it is built to be useful, not to keep you on your phone.

## Try it

Full access is free for 14 days after setup, with no payment needed to start. After that, intori is $8.99 a month or $79 a year.
